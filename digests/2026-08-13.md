### `feat/recon-by-meta` gains two new open PRs, first quiet merge-day in a while

After three merges landed in the previous digest, today is the mirror image: nothing closed, but two new PRs opened against the same branch — a bandwidth-usage improvement (`#7960`) and a small version bump pulling in a real WebSocket-reconnection fix from `mx-chain-communication-go` (`#7958`). Neither has review activity yet. Reads as the branch staying in steady incremental motion rather than stalling.

### A host-driver reliability fix surfaces from outside the watchlist

`mx-chain-communication-go #85` fixes a genuine bug: after a broken pipe, the WebSocket reconnect loop wasn't re-triggering because a failed close could leave the client thinking the connection was still live (a "zombie conn"). The fix closes on write error per the underlying library's own guidance, which unblocks the `Listen()` loop. This kind of fix is exactly what the org-wide sweep step exists to catch — it would otherwise be invisible since the repo isn't in the formal 14-repo list, but it directly affects the outport/host driver that `mx-chain-go`'s indexing pipeline depends on.

### Two tracked items are now stuck long enough to warrant direct follow-up

`#7951` (nonce-delta/sync-time config) at seven digests with zero movement, and mainnet-config `#259` at two digests static after its last mechanical merge-from-master — both are long past the point where passive daily re-checking adds new information.
