If `mx-chain-go` cuts another tag before devnet/testnet catch up, there will be three networks on three different versions simultaneously — worth flagging explicitly if that happens.

Worth watching whether this recurs or was a one-off (e.g., an urgent fix pushed outside normal hours — the commits landed 16:04–16:42Z, ordinary working hours).

5. **The chain's own health remains completely decoupled from the release/review churn above** — round cadence has been exactly on schedule across every window in this series, including the quiet Sep 28 gap and this window's release flurry. The interesting signal in this repo continues to be process and release cadence, not liveness.
