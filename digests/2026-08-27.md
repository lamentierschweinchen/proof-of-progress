## Themes

### The epoch-start-trigger stack lands its first two PRs, but the tip is growing faster than it's shrinking

After five straight digests of the chain only getting longer, `#7983` and `#7984` merged today — with real human sign-off, a first for this stack. That should read as progress, and structurally it is: two of the four tracked links are gone, folded into `#7971`. But the net effect on open surface area is close to a wash, because `#7991` — the PR that inherited the merged work as its new base — grew by 866 lines and 10 files in the same window, with its own visible review-and-fix cycle ("fixes after review," two rounds of "fix tests"). The chain is shorter (`#7971` ← `#7991` instead of `#7971` ← `#7983` ← `#7984` ← `#7991`) but the total diff being carried hasn't shrunk — it's just consolidated onto fewer, larger PRs. Worth reading as "the team is actively driving this to completion" rather than "this is stalling," given the review activity, but the size of `#7991` alone (2,466 lines added, 29 files, reaching into `processComponents.go` and `shardblockProposal.go`) still makes it the single largest open item in the watchlist.

### A second, separate front opens: p2p network telemetry ahead of the transition

`#7992` is notable less for its content — three commits adding message-size and quota stats to the p2p layer — and more for what it isn't: it's the first PR in over a week to target `rc/supernova` directly instead of stacking on the epoch-start-trigger branches. Draft status and the "5 min internal" commit message suggest this is early-stage instrumentation work, likely in support of monitoring the transition window itself once it goes live, rather than a fix for a known bug.
