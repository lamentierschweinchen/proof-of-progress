### The Supernova epoch-start-trigger stack extends to five PRs, and starts touching block-processing wiring

Three of the four non-root PRs in this stack moved today. `#7983` — the drain-window PR that already had a `GasProcessingPolicy` mechanism and a mini-block-syncer concurrency fix as of yesterday — picked up a fifth commit, "transition window readiness," without a title or scope change yet visible from the diff alone. More significant is the new top-of-stack PR, [#7991](https://github.com/multiversx/mx-chain-go/pull/7991) "trigger recovery hardening," which is the first PR in this chain to reach outside the epoch-start trigger package itself: it edits `factory/processing/processComponents.go` (the component-wiring factory that assembles the node's processing pipeline) and both `process/block/shardblock.go` and `shardblockProposal.go`, alongside new error definitions in `process/block/errors.go`. Its second commit, "add future epoch start meta header checks and notifier interface," landed at 12:02:48Z — essentially concurrent with this digest's research pass, so its full shape isn't yet reviewable. The pattern across all four stacked PRs remains consistent: `#7971` handles cross-header settlement checks, `#7983` handles drain-window gas-limit and mini-block-sync correctness, `#7984` adds finality-evidence retry to the trigger, and `#7991` now hardens trigger recovery and wires a notifier interface into block processing. All four are still unreviewed.
