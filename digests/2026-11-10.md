## Themes

### Contract framework release

- [v0.66.3](https://github.com/multiversx/mx-sdk-rs/releases/tag/v0.66.3) ships the gas-regression fix from [#2437](https://github.com/multiversx/mx-sdk-rs/pull/2437) as an opt-in feature. The PR traced the cost to how the VM charges `mBufferGetByteSlice` and `mBufferCopyByteSlice` for the whole source buffer; the named affected pattern is `SingleValueMapper<ManagedVec<...>>` as in mx-exchange-sc `token-unstake`.
- `rc/v0.67` now exists and has taken master plus [`sc-meta all check`](https://github.com/multiversx/mx-sdk-rs/pull/2440), a faster wasm-compile verification across a workspace.

### JS SDK `feat/next` and CI

- [#687](https://github.com/multiversx/mx-sdk-js-core/pull/687) is the second consecutive daily sync of `feat/next` with `main`; [#688](https://github.com/multiversx/mx-sdk-js-core/pull/688) updates CI and the pinned Node version.
