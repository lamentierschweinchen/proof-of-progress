## Themes

### The graduation PR's approval got wiped by its own downstream cascade, for the third time

[#7925](https://github.com/multiversx/mx-chain-go/pull/7925) went into this window `APPROVED`/`CLEAN` per yesterday's digest — the strongest state it had reached in nine days of tracking. This morning, #7917 (a dormant Jul 14 branch that finally merged) fed its merge commit into `recon-by-meta`, which cascaded into #7925's branch one second later. Both reviewers' Jul 27 approvals vanished, and the PR is back to `REVIEW_REQUIRED`/`BLOCKED`. The same thing happened around Jul 24, and again the base-branch merges keep resetting review state faster than reviewers can re-approve. Until the team either freezes the base branch ahead of merging #7925 or reviews land after the last feeder PR, "one merge-click away" reads as a moving target, not a milestone.
