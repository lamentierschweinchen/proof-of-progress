## Themes

### A second advisory-driven release cycle just concluded

Ten days ago this repo's digests were tracking `GHSA-hwg4-v7gq-phfc` as an embargoed, resolved advisory. This window surfaced a second one: `GHSA-xr93-fx88-vm63`, tracked via the same private-fork-replace-directive pattern in `go.mod`, first appearing around Jul 20 and iterating through four fork commits by Jul 23. It shipped today in `mx-chain-vm-go` v1.5.47 and `mx-chain-go` v1.11.10, and the fork override was cleanly removed from `go.mod` once the fix was upstream. The paired "new flag" / `AttributeExtraGasUsageEnableEpoch` changes (moved to epoch 1 in [#7946](https://github.com/multiversx/mx-chain-go/pull/7946)) tie the fix to attribute gas usage and safemath in the VM — consistent, mechanical evidence of what subsystem is affected, without speculating on the underlying vulnerability itself, which hasn't been publicly disclosed.

### The release is a real mainnet event, not routine housekeeping

[mx-chain-mainnet-config #272](https://github.com/multiversx/mx-chain-mainnet-config/pull/272) names an explicit mainnet activation epoch (2198) and date (Thursday Aug 6) in its release notes — a harder commitment than the usual "unchanged tag" cadence in this repo. Worth watching for confirmation as Aug 6 approaches and for any pre-activation testnet/devnet signal.

### The recon-by-meta chain absorbed the release without losing its own momentum

`feat/testnet-fixes` and `feat/recon-by-meta` both pulled in the day's release content within a few hours ([#7947](https://github.com/multiversx/mx-chain-go/pull/7947), [#7948](https://github.com/multiversx/mx-chain-go/pull/7948)), keeping the working branches current with mainline security fixes while the graduation work from Jul 29 continues to sit behind it.
